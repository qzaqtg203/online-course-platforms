## FAQ

**How much can I save using multi-carrier shipping software versus carrier retail rates?**

Commercial rates through shipping platforms typically save 50-89% off retail carrier pricing, with exact savings depending on service types and volumes. USPS Priority Mail savings reach 89% through platforms like Pirate Ship or Stamps.com, while UPS and FedEx discounts range 30-60% based on negotiated contracts. For a business shipping 100 packages monthly, savings often exceed the platform subscription costs within the first few shipments.

**Do I need my own carrier accounts or can I use platform-negotiated rates?**

Most platforms offer pre-negotiated carrier rates you can use immediately without establishing direct carrier accounts.
